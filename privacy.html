Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Homebridge Nest is a personal home-automation setup used only by its owner for their own home. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, Homebridge Nest uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read the status of the owner's Google Nest thermostats (such as temperature, humidity, mode, target temperature and heating or cooling status) and to change their settings.

How the data is used

This information is used only to display and control the thermostats in the owner's Apple Home app, through Homebridge running on the owner's own computer at home.

Sharing and storage

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not stored on any outside server operated by this app; it stays on the owner's home computer and within Google's and Apple's own services.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access) or from the Google Nest permissions page.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
